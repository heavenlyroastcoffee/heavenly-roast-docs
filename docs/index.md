## Which drinks hold up best at a hot outdoor event in Florida

Drinks that hold up best at a hot outdoor event in Florida are iced lattes, iced matcha, iced chai, and lemonade refreshers.

Avoid heavy hot-only menus in July and August. Lead with iced. The cart menu runs hot or iced on espresso, matcha, and chai, with dairy-free milk ready.

My cart needs about 6x6 ft of flat space and power even outdoors. Shade over that square matters. I arrive about 60 minutes early to ice bins.

## How do you keep milk, ice, and cups safe in Central Florida sun

Keep milk, ice, and cups safe in Central Florida sun with coolers, shade, and small batches. Milk stays in coolers until the cup is poured.

Ice needs double the plan you think. Outdoor events in Orlando burn through ice by noon. Hosts should confirm an ice run source nearby.

Cups need weight and cover. Keep cup stacks in bins, not on open tables.

## Where should the coffee cart stand at an outdoor venue

The coffee cart should stand on flat ground near shade, power, and foot traffic. Place the cart where guests pass naturally.

Face the menu board away from sun glare. Hang the board under tent shade.
